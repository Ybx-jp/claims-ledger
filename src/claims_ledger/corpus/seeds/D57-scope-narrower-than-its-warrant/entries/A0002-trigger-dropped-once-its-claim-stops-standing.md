## Assertion

A refresh trigger whose motivating claim is refuted, superseded or retracted is dropped
from the roster rather than reported against.

## Scope

metric: whether a trigger whose motivating claim is refuted, superseded or retracted is reported
cohort: triggers whose motivating claim is refuted, superseded or retracted
condition: the motivating claim has stopped standing

## Grounds

- entry: A0001-stale-fraction-law · cites-as-live

## Warrant

A report against such a trigger names a repair nobody can make: once the motivating claim
has reached a terminal status nothing further may be recorded against it, so the trigger
is rebuilt on whatever replaces the claim instead of being carried along with a flag.
